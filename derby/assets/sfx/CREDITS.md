# Auto Destruction sound clips

Every clip here was cut from Sonniss's free **GDC Game Audio Bundles** (https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc): the 2026 bundle, and for the real gunfire the 2016 (parts 4 and 6), 2017 (part 4), 2018 (part 3), 2020 (part 9) and 2024 (part 5) bundles from the same page. Each bundle is royalty free for commercial and non-commercial games, with no credit required; its licence does not allow passing on the raw library files, only using them inside a product. These are short trimmed, levelled, mono cuts made for this game, as Monster Mayhem's own `assets/sfx` are (see the repo's `LICENSES.md`).

### The weapons

The guns, the scattergun, the cannon and their mechanics are real recordings, one shot each, trimmed and faded (a round that was followed by another in the recording keeps its own first 45 to 75 ms and is crossfaded onto the clean tail of the last round of that burst). The rest are layered from the cuts below (times are seconds in the source file), pitched, filtered, enveloped and gently saturated. Pack names: **ESM** Epic Stock Media; **Booms** Federico Soler, Effective Trailer Booms Vol 2; **Fireworks** Ivo Vicic, Fireworks FX: Powerful Explosions Multiples In A Row Near; **344** 344 Audio; **IMA** InMotionAudio; **Dumais** David Dumais, Melee Weapons 2.
