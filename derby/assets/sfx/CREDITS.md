# Auto Destruction sound clips

Every clip here was cut from the **Sonniss GDC 2026 Game Audio Bundle** (https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc). The bundle is royalty free for commercial and non-commercial games, with no credit required; its licence does not allow passing on the raw library files, only using them inside a product. These are short trimmed, levelled, mono cuts made for this game, as Monster Mayhem's own `assets/sfx` are (see the repo's `LICENSES.md`).
